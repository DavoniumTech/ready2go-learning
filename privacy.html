YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Ready2Go Learning is a learning product from Davonium Technologies. This notice explains what information the app may use, where it is stored, and what to consider when using it.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account or sign in, the authentication service may process your email address, account identifier, sign-in provider and display name. The study plan can include your selected subjects, study category, preferred institution or course, state and intended admission year. You should not enter sensitive information that is not needed to study.

2. Practice history and device storage

Subject choices, theme preferences, question attempts and practice summaries are stored locally on your device so the app can continue working after content has been cached. Local records may be lost if you clear browser data, remove the app, reset your device or use a different browser/device. Use the app's export option where available before clearing local data.

3. Optional account and cloud services

When Firebase sign-in and the relevant server functions are configured, the study plan and limited practice-session summaries may be synchronised to the Ready2Go service. Cloud sync requires a network connection. Signing in is optional for the locally stored practice features described by the app.

4. Content delivery and technical information

When the app downloads question packs, the hosting provider and the Ready2Go content service may process ordinary connection details such as IP address, request time, browser type and requested resource to deliver and protect the service. The application does not send private R2 credentials to your browser.

5. Service providers

Depending on the features enabled, the service may use Firebase Authentication, Firebase Functions/Firestore, Cloudflare R2/Workers and the web hosting provider. Each provider processes data according to its own terms and applicable agreements.

6. Payments, advertising and automated tutoring

Payment processing, premium subscriptions, advertising, automated teacher-referral payouts and a live AI tutor are not currently part of the Ready2Go learning features described here. This notice must be updated before any such feature is introduced.

7. Young learners

Ready2Go is intended for students preparing for examinations. If you are under 18, review account and privacy decisions with a parent or guardian where appropriate. Do not share passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal details in the app.

8. Your choices and requests

You can change study preferences, use local practice without signing in, and clear local history where supported. Account, cloud-data deletion and retention requests may require help from the service operator. Contact options should be confirmed on the official Davonium Technologies website.

9. Security and changes

We use browser storage and configured service providers to support the features described above, but no internet service or device is risk-free. We may update this notice as the app changes. Check the date above for the latest published version.

10. Contact

For questions about Ready2Go, use the contact route available on the Davonium Technologies website.

This notice describes the current product design. The operator should obtain appropriate legal review and confirm contact, retention and request-handling arrangements before a broad public launch.
