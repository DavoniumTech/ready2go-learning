DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Prototype draft, not approved legal advice.

1. Who operates Ready2Go?

Ready2Go Learning is a learning product being developed by Davonium Technologies. The final operator/contact details must be confirmed before public launch.

2. Information used in this prototype

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, account identifier and profile display name. The prototype stores your selected study category, selected subjects, theme preference and completed practice sessions locally in your browser/device using IndexedDB (with a local storage fallback where needed).

3. What is not implemented yet

Cloud synchronisation, payments, teacher commission payouts, analytics and advertising are not implemented in this prototype. Do not treat this draft as a promise about services that have not been built.

4. Local storage and deletion

Local practice data may be lost if browser site data is cleared or the device is reset. The prototype provides an export and a way to clear local practice history. A production version should explain account deletion and any server-side retention process.

5. Question content

The included pilot questions are original Ready2Go practice questions and are not presented as official JAMB past questions.

6. Children and education data

Before launch, the operator must determine the applicable requirements for younger students, parental consent where required, lawful processing, security, retention and data-subject rights.

7. Contact

The intended support address is support@davoniumtech.com, which must be configured and verified before publication.

Important launch condition

This draft must be completed with the actual operating entity, contact details, retention policy, deletion process, service providers and applicable Nigerian legal requirements before public release. Obtain qualified legal review where appropriate.
