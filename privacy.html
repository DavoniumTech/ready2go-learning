Privacy Notice

Starter draft · Updated 16 October 2026 · Requires review and completion before public launch.

1. Who operates Ready2Go?

Ready2Go Learning is an educational product being developed by Davonium Technologies. For company information, visit davoniumtech.com. Before public launch, the operator must confirm the legally responsible entity and a monitored privacy-contact channel.

2. Information this build may process

If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, authentication identifier, sign-in provider and profile display name. Your study category, selected subjects, intended institution/course, theme preference, and practice history are stored on your device using IndexedDB (with a browser-storage fallback where available). If the optional cloud functions are deployed and you are signed in, a limited study-plan profile and practice-session summary may be synchronised to Firebase Firestore.

3. Question packs and Cloudflare R2

Published practice-pack JSON files may be delivered through a Ready2Go Cloudflare Worker connected to a private R2 bucket. The bucket's credentials are not sent to the app. The content delivery endpoint may receive standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested file and time as part of providing the service; confirm provider retention terms in the production privacy review.

4. Offline storage and device limits

Question packs and local progress are stored on your device. Clearing website data, uninstalling the PWA or resetting the device may remove local content and progress. Downloaded packs may occupy device storage. Cloud sync is optional and works only when the backend has been configured; do not assume local records are backed up.

5. What is not active in this build

Payment processing, premium access, advertising, analytics, automated teacher commission calculations and partner payouts are not active. This notice must be revised before any of them are enabled.

6. Educational guidance

Institution and course selections help organise your study plan. They are not official admission decisions. Verify course availability, subject combinations, O'Level requirements and current admission information through JAMB IBASS and the relevant institution.

7. Children and student information

Before launch, the operator must determine applicable requirements for younger users, parental or guardian consent where necessary, lawful processing, data minimisation, access/deletion requests, incident handling and retention. We will not ask learners to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information in the practice app.

8. Your choices

You can edit locally saved preferences, export local data where that feature is present, and clear local practice history. A production account deletion, server retention and request-handling workflow must be implemented and tested before this notice is treated as final.

9. Security and third parties

Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Functions/Firestore (when deployed), Cloudflare R2 and a compatible hosting provider may support the service. No public front-end file should contain secret R2 credentials or a service-account key. Providers process information under their own terms and applicable agreements.

10. Contact and complaints

Use the contact route on the Davonium Technologies website once it is verified and monitored. Add a dedicated privacy contact before launch.

Important launch condition

This document is a product-specific starter draft, not legal advice or a statement of legal compliance.
