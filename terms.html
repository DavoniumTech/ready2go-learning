Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Prototype draft, not approved legal advice.

1. Educational purpose

Ready2Go Learning is a practice and revision tool. It does not guarantee exam scores, admission or any particular outcome. Students should verify examination and course requirements against official JAMB and institution materials.

2. Question sources

Questions labelled “Ready2Go Original” are intended to be original practice content, not official JAMB examination questions. Past-question content may only be added where there is permission or another valid legal basis for use.

3. Accounts

Users must provide accurate account information, keep credentials secure and not attempt to access another person's account or data.

4. Prototype limitations

This prototype is for testing. Question coverage is limited; certain subjects are listed but their practice banks are not yet available. Local progress is stored on the device and is not yet synchronised to an account.

5. Acceptable use

Do not abuse the service, attempt to bypass security, redistribute material unlawfully, or interfere with other users.

6. Future paid features

Paid plans, refunds, premium entitlements and teacher referral commissions are not active in this prototype. Separate clear terms must be published before those programmes begin.

7. Contact and governing law

Contact: support@davoniumtech.com (to be configured). The operator must confirm the applicable governing law, complaint process and dispute-resolution terms before launch.

Important launch condition
