Teacher Partner Terms

Draft framework · Updated 16 October 2026 · Do not rely on it until the programme is formally activated.

1. Individual teachers only

The planned programme is for individual teachers who voluntarily choose to recommend Ready2Go. It is not a school partnership or school endorsement programme. A teacher must not claim a school officially endorses Ready2Go without written permission.

2. No current commissions or payouts

In this build, referral links, verified-sale commission calculations and payout processing are not active. This page does not promise a commission, rate or income. A signed final agreement and an active system are required before referrals can earn money.

3. Future attribution rules

Before launch, Ready2Go must publish the referral attribution window, eligible sale definition, refund/chargeback treatment, duplicate or fraudulent referral rules, reporting delays, commission rate, payout threshold, payout timing and appeal route. Student registration alone should not create a commission.

4. Honest promotion

Partners must not make false claims about question counts, official JAMB affiliation, guaranteed exam scores/admission, earnings or institutional endorsement. Do not post students' personal information or use school marks without permission.

5. Payment and legal checks

Any commission ledger must be created only after a server-verified eligible payment event. Payouts require an appropriate lawful provider, verified beneficiary details and compliance review. Client-side events are not proof of payment.

6. Activation

This planned agreement must be completed with the actual business identity, contact channel, final commercial terms, privacy details and applicable legal review before a teacher is enrolled.
