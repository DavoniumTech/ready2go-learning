CONTENT QUALITY

Content, sources and corrections

Starter policy · Updated 16 October 2026 · Requires review before public release.

Original question labels

“Ready2Go Original” identifies a question authored for this product. It does not mean the question is an official JAMB item or that the item has already passed final academic review. Each question should carry a subject, topic, difficulty, answer, explanation, source type and review status.

Official past questions

We will mark an item as a past question only when its source and year can be reliably verified and use is lawful. No complete 2020 JAMB question bank is included in this starter. Do not upload copied question sets or a textbook unless appropriate permission or another valid legal basis has been confirmed.

Set texts

Book title metadata is not the same as permission to reproduce the book. The starter does not contain the full text of The Lekki Headmaster. Verify the official prescribed text for the relevant exam year and use only authorised or otherwise lawfully usable material.

Review and corrections

Before publication, each item should be checked for correctness, syllabus fit, clear wording, unambiguous options, correct answer key, useful explanation, appropriate difficulty and rights status. Provide a monitored correction route through the Davonium Technologies website before the public launch.

Admission guidance

JAMB IBASS and relevant institution materials are the sources of truth for programmes and subject combinations. Do not publish an estimated or historical cut-off as current without institution, programme, admission-year, source and verification-date metadata.
