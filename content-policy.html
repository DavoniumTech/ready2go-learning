LEARNING QUALITY

Content, sources and corrections

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Practice material

Ready2Go contains original practice questions written for learning and revision. An original question is not an official JAMB question. A past question should only be labelled with an exam and year when the original source and permission to use it have been verified.

Answer explanations

Explanations are intended to show the reasoning behind an answer, not merely reveal a letter. Educational content can contain mistakes or be improved. Learners should compare important facts and formulae against trusted resources and tell us when an explanation seems unclear or incorrect.

Review and corrections

Questions should be checked for factual correctness, one unambiguous correct option, appropriate distractors, clear wording, syllabus alignment, answer-key accuracy and useful explanations before being treated as verified. A correction should identify the subject and question so that the content can be located.

Copyright and source labels

Book title metadata is not permission to reproduce a book. Questions, passages, diagrams, set texts and third-party explanations are included only when their source, licence or other lawful basis has been confirmed. Do not upload copied question banks or protected material without the necessary rights.

Course and admission information

JAMB and the relevant institution are the authoritative sources for current requirements. Course directories and general guidance in Ready2Go are intended to help you discover what to check; they do not establish that a course is currently offered, that a subject combination is accepted, or that an applicant is eligible.

Report a correction

Use the contact route on the official Davonium Technologies website to report unclear wording, a suspected wrong answer or a catalogue detail that needs review.
